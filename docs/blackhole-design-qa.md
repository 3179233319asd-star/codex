The user's latest feedback was that many details still differed. This review supersedes the earlier visual pass. The darker, narrow lower lens and orderly luminous ribbons were substantive fidelity issues.

## Changes from the previous version

1. Retained the central shadow and photon rim while spreading only the outer gas image. Expansion is reduced near the disc plane so the foreground does not become too tall.
2. Replaced a single emission surface with three thin, independently textured gas strata. Ray crossings composite in distance order with weighted opacity, giving the foreground depth and occlusion.
3. Added drifting scattered gas with silver-blue outer wisps, warm inner light, irregular gaps and sparse knots. This fills the missing broad lower halo without enlarging the core.
4. Reduced excessive approaching-side brightness, softened the inner cut, used neutral ivory highlights and separated the near-side outer falloff from the farther gas.
5. Concentrated the upper hot band and lowered peripheral exposure. Added faint broken wing gas and foreground dust; fixed initially regular dotted rows with sparse, jittered glints.
6. Restored point-like peripheral stars, varied color/brightness, and added selected bright-star positions and two procedurally generated galaxies. Dust clouds have broken, warped envelopes.
7. Added optical filtering and photon-line antialiasing. Avoided repeated rendering from identical ResizeObserver bounds, retaining GPU fences and adaptive resolution.
8. Hid the tiny English decorative eyebrow on portrait phones, where the expanded halo reduced its contrast. The Chinese title and controls remain visible.

## Visual comparison

The source and final scene were opened together at full-frame and focused-crop scale. A second read-only review checked the shadow, crossing disc, lens images and starfield.

- Geometry: the core remains around x=640, y=343 with a thin rim of roughly 140 px radius, close to the reference. The foreground obscures the lower core. The cool lower halo now extends to roughly y=610, rather than ending near y=550.
- Lighting: the upper hot band is concentrated, the broad lower image and foreground veil are present, and the left side no longer has excessive brightness. Selected stars have small cores with restrained halos.
- Typography/layout: locally bundled Manrope/Noto Sans SC and official Phosphor Light icons remain intact. Desktop and landscape controls fit. Portrait copy sits over dim outer gas, with the low-contrast decorative eyebrow removed.
- Assets: all gas, stars and galaxies are generated by code. No background photograph, raster texture or video is used. Fonts are bundled locally.

A mean sRGB luma proxy in the central x=570–710 strip supports the lighting comparison. Values range from 0 to 1; this is image comparison, not an astrophysical measurement.

| Area / image rows | Reference | Final |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Upper outer gas / 65–120 | 0.216 | 0.178 |
| Upper hot band / 120–170 | 0.625 | 0.658 |
| Foreground veil / 392–445 | 0.158 | 0.159 |
| Lower inner band / 470–525 | 0.545 | 0.590 |
| Lower outer gas / 570–620 | 0.156 | 0.176 |

Residual limits remain visible: the reference has more individually tangled fine threads and irregular warm glints; the generated arch and foreground are somewhat smoother and more parallel. The lower bright image is slightly cooler and broader. Exact dust/star patterns differ. This is a refined moving approximation, not a claim of pixel-identical reproduction or an exact relativistic simulation.
